The actual ingress/proxy duration must be checked before treating a long synchronous call as usable. A live connection also requires an approved credential-bearing Router origin, caller authorization, provider/model binding, immutable deployed source identity, and a witnessed completion/receipt. Those bindings were not established. Each integrity repair needs its cited authorization and regression contract; the current findings alone do not authorize a production security change.
